GOOD TO KNOW

Common questions

Does this course cover the whole examination?

No. Aptitude Mind Plus focuses on the aptitude, numerical, verbal and reasoning portions relevant to each exam. Other examination subjects are not included.

Is the free learning section really free?

Yes. Videos, concepts, exercises and approved questions are open to all. Only optional personal tutoring is paid; applying does not take payment.

Are recorded lessons or certificates included?

Lessons appear only after real videos are published. Certificates and recorded tutoring sessions are not promised.

Can I try something for free first?

Yes. Visit our free units-digit concept lesson and original eight-question practice quiz. No account is required.
