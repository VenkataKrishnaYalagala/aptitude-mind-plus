Units digit and cyclicity

See why powers repeat in short cycles, then try an original eight-question quiz.

Read lesson notes

The last digits of powers of 7 follow a four-step cycle: 7, 9, 3, 1. To find the units digit of 7^2026, divide 2026 by 4. The remainder is 2, so use the second digit in the cycle: 9. This works because multiplying by 7 advances the repeating units-digit pattern.
